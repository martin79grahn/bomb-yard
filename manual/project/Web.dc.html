Bomb Yard also runs in any web browser, on a computer or a phone. Before you start, pick a look in the menu. Your choice is remembered for next time. The rules are the same in both.

CLASSIC MODE The original pixel art and the same chiptune songs as the NES cart. Bit faces two monsters in each level, the same as on the NES.
NEW ENHANCED MODE 3D blocks seen at a tilt, glowing fire, shadows and screen shake. New monsters and modern music.

Enhanced music, level by level: tropical house, desert deep house, ambient synthwave, a dark dirge with a tolling bell, then organ and choir.

ENHANCED MONSTERS

WANDERERS Krax, yetis, slimes and fish roam about.
CHARGERS Asps and beetles charge down corridors at you.
HUNTERS Imps, angels and wisps fly after you.

Every level has its own creatures, and every boss is new.
