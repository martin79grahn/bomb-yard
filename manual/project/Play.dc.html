HOW TO PLAY

Blast both monsters to clear the level. Bombs send fire straight out in four directions. Fire burns for one second and stops at the first block it hits. Careful: your own fire hurts you too!

LEVEL Which part of the quarry you are in.
B F S R Your bombs, fire range and skates, and R while you hold the remote.
TIME Two minutes per level. At 0:00 the roof caves in.
MONSTERS The level's monster and how many are still loose.
Concrete. Can't be blasted.
Stone. Cracks open and drops a power-up.
Bomb. Nobody can walk through it.
Fire. Sets off other bombs it touches.
0:20 When 20 seconds are left the music speeds up. In the last 10 seconds the clock blinks. Hurry!
