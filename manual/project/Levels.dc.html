THE LEVELS

Every level is a brand-new random quarry with its own creatures, quicker the deeper Bit goes. After every level waits a boss from that level.

LEVEL 1 THE YARD Green grass at the top of the quarry, home of the Krax. Their crowned king is the boss.
LEVEL 2 SAND PIT A hot, dry pit. Asps rear up out of the sand, and a giant scorpion waits.
LEVEL 3 FROST CAVE An icy cave full of little yetis, guarded by a woolly mammoth.
LEVEL 4 LAVA PEAK Smoking rock and glowing cracks. Fire imps fly about, and a dragon rules.
LEVEL 5 CLOUD NINE Golden gates above the clouds. Angels, and a storm cloud full of lightning.
LEVEL 6 DEEP SEA Coral on the sea floor. Clownfish, and a shark for a boss.
BOSS
AFTER THE SHARK THE FINAL BOSS The green bomber, back in the Yard. Each boss is tougher: 3 to 8 hits, more bombs, longer blasts. Halfway it turns angry.
