THE LEVELS

Every level is a brand-new random quarry. The deeper Bit goes, the faster the Krax get. After every level waits a boss.

LEVEL 1 THE YARD Green grass at the top of the quarry. The Krax are still sleepy.
BOSS
AFTER EACH LEVEL THE BOSSES A bomber in the colours of the level before. Each is tougher: 3 to 7 hits, more bombs, longer blasts, more speed. Halfway it turns red and angry.
LEVEL 2 SAND PIT A dry pit further down. The heat makes the Krax run faster.
LEVEL 3 FROST CAVE The icy cave deep inside, where the Krax pick up speed.
LEVEL 4 LAVA PEAK Smoking rock and glowing cracks. Dark, dreadful music and even quicker Krax.
LEVEL 5 CLOUD NINE Golden gates above the clouds, to an organ and a choir.
LEVEL 6 DEEP SEA Coral and rock on the sea floor, waves and bubbles. Clownfish instead of Krax, and a shark for a boss.
