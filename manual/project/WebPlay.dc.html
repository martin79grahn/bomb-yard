WEB CONTROLS

KEYS + TOUCH

In the menu, choose 1P VS MONSTERS, 2 PLAYERS or DEBUG (1 player, walk through walls). The yard is bigger on the web: 16 by 16 squares.

1 PLAYER Arrow keys or W A S D to walk. Space or X drops a bomb.
PLAYER 1 In 2 players: W A S D and Space.
PLAYER 2 In 2 players: arrow keys and Enter.
P / ESC, M Pause and resume. M turns the sound on or off.
DRAG New enhanced mode: drag the arena with a finger or the mouse to tilt it. Double-tap or double-click resets.
ON A PHONE Use the arrows and the BOMB button on the screen. With 2 players, lay the phone flat between you: player 2's buttons sit at the top, turned to face across the table. 2 PLAYER MATCH First to 3 rounds wins. Each round moves to the next level, from The Yard to the Sand Pit to the Frost Cave. No monsters.
