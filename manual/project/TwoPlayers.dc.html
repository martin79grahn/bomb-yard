TWO PLAYERS

While the Krax sleep, Bit's cousin Red challenges him to a bomb duel. Plug in controller 2, choose 2 PLAYERS on the title screen and press START.

LEVEL Six arenas, from The Yard to the Deep Sea.
MONSTERS On: two of the level's monsters roam the arena, and whoever touches one is out.
WINS Rounds needed to win the match: 3, 5 or 10.
ITEMS Bomb-ups, fire-ups and skates per arena: 1 to 7 of each.
P1 / P2 Your bomber: Bit, Ruby, Sky or Nova. You can't both pick the same one.

Up/down picks a line, left/right changes it. START plays, B goes back.

  • Catch the other bomber in your blast to win the round.
  • Both out at once, or time runs out? It's a draw and nobody scores.
  • Every round is a new arena, and power-ups start over.
  • The top of the screen shows wins, like P1 2/5.
