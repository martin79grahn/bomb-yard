THE STORY

Nobody has worked the old quarry for a hundred years. All that is left are concrete pillars, piles of stone, and a crate of bombs the stonecutters forgot.

But the stone piles hid eggs. One night they hatched, and out came the Krax: small, angry birds with orange beaks that waddle about knocking over everything they meet. Now they rule the whole quarry.

BIT
has lived at the edge of the quarry all his life. He finds the bomb crate and sets out to clear the Krax, one part of the quarry at a time.

There is a catch. The quarry is built on old blasting rock that starts to rumble the moment a bomb goes off. In each part of the quarry Bit has exactly two minutes before the roof caves in.

Help Bit blast every Krax out of The Yard, the Sand Pit, the Frost Cave, Lava Peak, Cloud Nine and the Deep Sea!
