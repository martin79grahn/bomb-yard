WINNING

Clear all six levels and beat all seven bosses to see the CONGRATULATIONS! screen. Press START to play again.

Caught by a monster, burned in a blast, or out of time? You try the same level again on a new quarry.

DEBUG MODE In 1 player mode, press SELECT on the title screen until DEBUG ON shows. Bit can then walk straight through every wall and can't be hurt by fire, monsters or the clock.

BIT'S TIPS

  1. Plan your escape before you drop a bomb. Fire only goes in straight lines, so duck around a corner.
  2. Blast stone early. Extra bombs and fire make the monsters much easier.
  3. Let the monsters come to you. Drop a bomb in the hallway they are heading into, then step away.
  4. Watch out for chain reactions. One bomb can set off the next sooner than you think.
