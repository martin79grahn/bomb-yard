MEGA DRIVE

16-BIT

Bomb Yard is on the Sega Mega Drive too, with the same rules as on the NES. Everything you see and hear is new.

GRAPHICS Shaded pixel art. Blocks cast shadows, fire flickers and bombs flash red before they blow. Each level has its own colours.
MUSIC The NES tunes rearranged for the FM synth, plus new title and boss songs.
SOUND Sampled effects: deep explosions, a sparkle for the heart, a clang for the boss.

The ROM is md/bomb-yard.bin and runs in any Mega Drive emulator. Player 2 uses controller 2.
