POWER-UPS

Bit starts with 1 bomb and a blast of 1 square. Every level hides bomb-ups, fire-ups and skates, 1 to 7 of each (5 unless you change ITEMS on the options screen), and, with 1 player, 1 heart under its stones. Bombs and fire have no limit, and you keep your power-ups from level to level.

BOMB-UP One more bomb on the field at a time.
FIRE-UP Your blast reaches one square further.
SKATE Walk 25% faster. Up to 4 skates, for double speed.
PENETRATOR Blasts go through stone and break every block in reach. 2 per level; lost if you die.
REMOTE Bombs wait until you press B, then all go off. 2 per level; lost if you die.
