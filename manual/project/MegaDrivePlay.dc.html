MD CONTROLS

3-BUTTON PAD
D-PAD Walk. Title: 1 player, 2 players or boss rush
A B C Drop a bomb (any of the three)
START Start, pause and play on
B Options screen: go back
C Title screen: debug mode, walk through walls, can't die

On the options screen, pick your bomber: Bit, Ruby, Sky or Nova, each in their own colours. With 1 player or boss rush there is also DEBUG: walk through walls and never die.

THE SCREEN

Left panel: your bombs (an arrowed bomb once you hold the penetrator), blast range, skates and lives. Right panel: Krax left, the boss's hits to go, or player 2. Top: the level and the clock, which turns yellow in the last 20 seconds while the music speeds up.

THE BOSSES

One waits after every level, dressed in its colours. Each is tougher than the last: 3 to 6 hits, more bombs, a longer blast and more speed. Halfway it turns angry, redder and faster. Its own bombs can't hurt it, only yours can.
