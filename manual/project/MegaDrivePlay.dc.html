MD CONTROLS

3-BUTTON PAD
D-PAD Walk. Title: 1 player, 2 players or boss rush
A B C Drop a bomb (any of the three). With the remote, B sets your bombs off
START Start, pause and play on
B Options screen: go back
C Title screen: debug mode, walk through walls, can't die

On the options screen, pick your bomber: Bit, Ruby, Sky or Nova, each in their own colours. With 1 player or boss rush there is also DEBUG: walk through walls and never die.

THE SCREEN

Left panel: your bombs (an arrowed bomb once you hold the penetrator), blast range, skates, lives and, at the bottom, the remote while you hold it. Right panel: monsters left (FOES), the boss's hits to go, or player 2. Top: the level and the clock, which turns yellow in the last 20 seconds while the music speeds up.

THE BOSSES

A creature from each level: the Krax King, a scorpion, a mammoth, a dragon, a storm cloud, the shark, and last the green bomber, back in the Yard. Each is tougher: 3 to 8 hits, more bombs, a longer blast and more speed. Halfway it turns angry. Only your bombs hurt it.
