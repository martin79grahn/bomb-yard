MD CONTROLS

3-BUTTON PAD
D-PAD Walk. In the menus: choose
A B C Drop a bomb (any of the three)
START Start, pause and play on
B Options screen: go back
C Title screen: debug mode, walk through walls

On the options screen, pick your bomber: Bit, Ruby, Sky or Nova, each in their own colours.

THE SCREEN

Left panel: your bombs, blast range, skates and lives. Right panel: Krax left, the boss's hits to go, or player 2. Top: the level and the clock, which turns yellow in the last 20 seconds while the music speeds up.

THE BOSS

Waits between levels 1 and 2. Drops 2 bombs at a time with a blast of 5. Three hits beat it; after two it turns red, runs faster and drops 3 bombs. Its own bombs can't hurt it, only yours can.
